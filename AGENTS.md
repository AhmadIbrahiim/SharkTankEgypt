# Writing

Every piece of user-facing text must sound natural and human in English and Arabic. Run a Humanizer pass before publishing new or edited copy.

Apply this to profiles, descriptions, FAQs, headings, buttons, notices, image text and metadata. Use the `humanizer` skill when available and follow the [writing guidance in CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#writing).

Read each language on its own. Prefer concrete details, ordinary words and varied sentence lengths. Remove filler, inflated claims and repetitive templates. Keep names, figures, dates, sources and uncertainty accurate; never invent a quote, opinion or personal experience to make prose sound human.
