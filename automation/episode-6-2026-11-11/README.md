## Where the terms come from

**Asks.** Youm7 ran two articles per pitch on the night of the broadcast: Wady Le3b ([ask](https://www.youm7.com/7615243), [services](https://www.youm7.com/7615245)), DTX ([ask](https://www.youm7.com/7615248), [production plan](https://www.youm7.com/7615249)) and Go Fresh ([ask](https://www.youm7.com/7615213), [expansion](https://www.youm7.com/7615215)). All three asks match the maintainer's account.

**Outcomes.** The maintainer watched the episode and supplied the three outcomes as a firsthand account on 12 November 2026. Youm7 reports no offers or outcomes, and the episode was not independently rewatched. The maintainer wrote “Hana + Ayman” for DTX. Season 5 has no shark named Hana, so the record uses Hala El-Kasem, and the maintainer confirmed that reading the same day. No conditions beyond amount and equity were supplied, so none are recorded.

**Video.** The official channel posted the [Wady Le3b pitch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=idJ-tuHYMLA) (21:47) and an [Episode 6 summary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lo7-hcWCzRc) (40:00) on 11 November. Neither was transcribed. DTX and Go Fresh had no pitch video yet.

## Findings to carry forward

**Wady Le3b.** Founder and CEO Mohamed Amr, per Youm7; the App Store lists “Mohamed Amr” as developer and Google Play lists “Dido Amr”. Youm7 mentions a partner without naming them. Downloads, sales, margins, forecasts and the EGP 600 million monthly market figure are the founder's on-air claims. Google Play showed 1K+ downloads and 8 reviews on 12 November; the App Store showed 4.7 from 30 ratings. The website's footer social icons have no links, and its legal pages refer to another product, “Virtual Expo”. A June 2026 SceneNow feature timed out on every attempt and was read only through search excerpts (launch in September 2025; matches in New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, Maadi and Nasr City). It is not cited in the profile. `founded: 2025` rests on the App Store's first version, dated 15 August 2025.

**DTX.** Founders Nashwa Emad and Mohamed Abdel Azim, married, 50% each, per Youm7. On air they said DTX started about five years ago; the company's about page says “10+ years in operation”. The profile uses the on-air statement, attributes it, and sets no founding year. The website gives “908 Industrial Area, New Cairo”; a search excerpt showed a different industrial-zone address, which was not found on the rendered site. The contact page's phone number is a placeholder (+20 123 456 789). The show's post tags a “DTX Printing” Facebook page whose URL was not captured. Two FAQ answers (fabric types and minimum order) did not render.

**Go Fresh.** Founders Youssef Wael and Riad El-Helw, per Youm7; the company's site names no founders. Halal certification, Egyptian Drug Authority certification, “Egypt's first” and “the world's only” are company claims; the profile attributes the certifications and leaves out the superlatives. The patent is the founders' on-air statement. Prices on the store match Youm7. The about page says the next batch will use wheat straw; that is a plan and is not recorded.

**Founder portraits.** Mohamed Amr, Nashwa Emad and Mohamed Abdel Azim use 320 × 320 crops of the official cards, credited to the show. Youm7's captioned stills of the same broadcast name Mohamed Amr alone and name the DTX pair together, where Nashwa Emad is the woman and Mohamed Abdel Azim the man. No portrait from the founders' own profiles was usable: Nashwa Emad's [LinkedIn photo](https://eg.linkedin.com/in/dtxprinting) is 100 px. Youssef Wael and Riad El-Helw have no portraits, because no source says which of the two people on the Go Fresh card is which. Go Fresh's site, Instagram bio and LinkedIn page name neither founder.
