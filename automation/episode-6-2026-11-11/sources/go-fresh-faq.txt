FAQ
What exactly is a Go Fresh Mini?

A disposable, waterless mini toothbrush. It's individually wrapped, finger-sized, and has a built-in bead that releases mint mouthwash flavor when you bite down — plus a toothpick end and tongue scraper on the back.

Do I need water or toothpaste to use it?

No. Go Fresh Minis are designed to work completely waterless — just open the wrapper and use it anywhere.

Is Go Fresh Halal certified?

Yes. Go Fresh Minis are Halal certified, and you'll find the Halal mark on every individual wrapper.

Is Go Fresh approved by the Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA)?

Yes. Go Fresh Minis are the only disposable toothbrush in Egypt certified by the Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA).

What does "Halal bead" mean?

The mint-flavored bead inside each brush is the first of its kind in the world made with Halal-certified ingredients.

Who is Go Fresh for?

Anyone who wants fresh breath on the go — before a date, after a meal with strong flavors like garlic or lamb, during travel, at the gym, between meetings, or if you wear braces and need a quick, easy way to clean up during the day.

How long does one Mini last?

Each Mini is designed for a single use — freshen up, then dispose of it. That's the whole point: no carrying a toothbrush and toothpaste around.

Is Go Fresh available outside Egypt?

We currently ship within Egypt. Follow @getgofresh for updates as we expand.

Where can I buy Go Fresh in person?

Look for our vending machines in some of Egypt's top malls, with more locations opening throughout 2026.

Is it safe for daily use?

Yes. Go Fresh Minis are meant for regular on-the-go freshening, though they're not a replacement for your regular brushing routine with a standard toothbrush and toothpaste.

What packs are available?

Go Fresh Minis come in packs of 6, 12, and 24, so you can pick what fits your routine — from occasional travel use to daily on-the-go freshness.
