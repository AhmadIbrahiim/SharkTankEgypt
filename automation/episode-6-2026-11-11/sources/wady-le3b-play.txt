About this app
arrow_forward
Welcome to Wady Le3b – your premier football community and pitch booking app.

Whether you're looking to book a field for your friends, join exciting local football matches, or compete in tournaments, Wady Le3b makes it effortless.

Key Features:
• Pitch Booking: Discover and reserve top-rated football pitches near you with instant confirmation.
• Join & Create Matches: Easily find open games to play in or organize your own match with friends.
• Tournaments & Leagues: Register teams, follow match schedules, and track tournament brackets.
• Player Profiles & Stats: Keep track of match statistics, player performance, and ratings.
• In-App Chat: Coordinate game details and communicate smoothly with your teammates.

Download Wady Le3b today and elevate your football experience!
