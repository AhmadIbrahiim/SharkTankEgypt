⚽ About Wady Le3b

Wady Le3b is Egypt's first football platform designed to give every player — from casuals to competitors — the experience of a pro footballer.

Through organized pickup games, real-time stats, public player profiles, and high-stakes tournaments, we're building a digital football community where anyone can play, shine, and get a glimpse of a true football professional.

You don't need a club. You don't need a team.
Just show up, play, and become part of something bigger.
