About Go Fresh

Go Fresh Minis is Egypt's first disposable, waterless toothbrush — and the world's only disposable toothbrush made with a Halal freshening bead.

Go Fresh Minis are Halal certified and the only disposable toothbrush in Egypt certified by the Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA). Every Mini meets the standards you'd expect from something you put in your mouth.

Each Mini is finger-sized and individually wrapped, so you can freshen up anytime, anywhere — no water, no toothpaste, no sink required. Snap the bead built into the brush to release a burst of mint mouthwash flavor, use the built-in toothpick end and tongue scraper, and you're done in seconds.

We spent 8 months in R&D to get the design right, and we're not stopping there — our next production batch will be made from wheat straw and fully recyclable, as part of our commitment to building Egypt's first global brand focused on on-the-go hygiene.

You'll find Go Fresh Minis vending machines in some of Egypt's biggest malls, and thousands of packs already in the hands of customers who reach for us before dates, meetings, flights, workouts, and everyday moments where fresh breath matters.
