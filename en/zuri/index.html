In short: ZURI pitched in Season 3, episode 14. They walked away with a deal with Ayman Abbas.

Overview

ZURI is an Egyptian bag brand whose name means "beautiful" in an African language. It designs light, practical everyday bags for people who want something stylish that is also easy to carry all day. Every piece is produced in local Egyptian workshops, which supports local craftsmanship. The brand is led by its founder, an engineer who is also its designer, and sells through its Cairo-based Instagram and Facebook pages. On the show it closed a deal with Ayman Abbas.

The pitch

ZURI's founder, an engineer who also designs the bags, pitched her light everyday bags made in Egyptian workshops. She asked for EGP 3 million for 15% of the company. The pitch drew five offers from the sharks.
