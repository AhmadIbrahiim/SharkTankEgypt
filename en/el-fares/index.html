In short: El Fares pitched in Season 3, episode 3. The founders asked for 5M EGP for 15%, valuing the company at 33.3M EGP. They walked away with a deal: 6.3M EGP for 50%.

Overview

El Fares for Agricultural Development is an Egyptian agri-biotech business that produces plant seedlings through tissue culture, a lab cloning technique. Farmers often plant stock that carries disease, which cuts yields. El Fares grows plantlets from clean mother plants under sterile conditions and multiplies them in large numbers, so growers get uniform, disease-free seedlings. It also sells to farmers online through a storefront on the Agrikoshk agricultural marketplace.

  • Uses tissue culture to mass-produce disease-free seedlings
  • Serves Egyptian farmers with healthier planting stock
  • Sells online via a storefront on the Agrikoshk marketplace
  • Closed a Shark Tank Egypt deal: EGP 5M for 50% (Season 3)

The pitch

El Fares pitched its tissue-culture approach to producing disease-free seedlings at scale and asked for EGP 5 million for 15% of the company. The episode ended with all five sharks joining one deal: EGP 6.25 million for 50%, more than three times the stake the founders first offered.

Services & products

  • Tissue-culture seedlingsLab-cloned, disease-free plant seedlings mass-produced from healthy mother plants for commercial farms.
  • Healthy planting stock for farmersUniform, true-to-type seedlings that give growers a cleaner, more reliable start than conventional nursery stock.
  • Online seedling storeFarmers can browse and order the company's products through its storefront on the Agrikoshk marketplace.
