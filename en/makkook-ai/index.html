In short: Makkook AI pitched in Season 5, episode 7. The founders asked for 35M EGP for 5%, valuing the company at 700M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Ayman Abbas and Ahmed Tarek Khalil: 35M EGP for 7%.

Overview

Makkook AI builds DIOS, software that helps factories, retailers and logistics companies plan their daily operations. It connects to systems such as ERP and SCADA, takes a company's goals and limits, and works out production schedules, stock levels, delivery routes and staffing. The company has offices in Cairo and Manama, Bahrain.

  • Offices in Cairo and Manama. Clutch lists 10–49 staff, most of them in Cairo.
  • DIOS is sold as an annual licence per engine plus a one-time setup fee. The company says it can go live in about three months.
  • Published case studies cover hypermarket stock planning, production planning for a glass maker and predicting logistics delays.
  • A founding member of the ManuTech Alliance, an industrial technology group, according to Gulf Prime Magazine.

The pitch

In Season 5, Episode 7, co-founder and CEO Mahmoud Hegab asked for EGP 35 million for 5% of Makkook AI, valuing it at EGP 700 million. He described a food factory handling about 100 orders a week, where production, warehousing, purchasing, finance and sales all pull in different directions, and said DIOS turns the whole company into one mathematical model to find the best plan. The valuation drew pushback. One shark argued that general AI tools such as Copilot and Claude could already do this kind of work. Hegab answered that language models give approximate answers, while factories and supply chains need exact ones, and that client data stays on the client's own servers. Ahmed Tarek defended the approach. Hegab named Kinaxis and o9 Solutions among about five global competitors and said Makkook has none in the Middle East. Ayman Abbas and Ahmed Tarek's final offer was EGP 35 million for 7%, valuing Makkook at EGP 500 million, plus a further 13% in advisory shares that falls to 8% once the company reaches a $20 million valuation. He accepted.

Services & products
