Overview

BASQ is a dessert brand from founders Mohamed Hamama and Mariam Taha with a single item on its menu: San Sebastián (Basque burnt) cheesecake. It sells the cake in three sizes, 12 cm, 20 cm and 24 cm. The business started in the founders' home kitchen and grew into a shop after restaurants began ordering in bulk. In Season 3 they asked the sharks for EGP 3 million for 10%. On the show it closed a deal with Nour Swid and Abdallah Sallam and Mohamed Farouk for EGP 3 million for 25%.

  • Single-item menu: San Sebastián cheesecake
