In short: O2 Volta pitched in Season 5, episode 8. The founders asked for 500K EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 5M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Mohamed Farouk: 1M EGP for 30%.

Overview

O2 Volta sells portable bottles of 99% pure oxygen, aimed at athletes and anyone after a lift in energy and focus. The brand, run under the name VOLTA Supplements, describes itself as the first recreational oxygen supplement in Egypt and the Middle East. It sells single bottles and multi-packs online and refills returned bottles.

  • On 25 November 2026 the online store listed one bottle at EGP 495, two at EGP 890 and three at EGP 1,275.
  • A returned bottle could be refilled for EGP 250 on the same date.

The pitch

In Season 5, Episode 8, Mohamed El-Mahdy, Abdulrahman El-Shobokshy and Ahmed El-Shobokshy asked for EGP 500,000 for 10% of their company, which the press coverage calls Volta Pharmac, valuing it at EGP 5 million. Their first product, O2 Volta, is a bottle holding 12 litres of 99% pure oxygen that sells for EGP 495. They said it helps with recovery and focus and is not a medical treatment. They reported EGP 1.58 million in revenue in seven months, helped by a campaign built on the line “we sold air in a bottle”, with a 55% gross margin, a 35% net margin and 10% to 15% of customers buying again. The product sells online, through a supplement chain with 90 branches and in pharmacies. They aim for at least EGP 2 million by the end of 2026 and EGP 10 million in 2027, and showed a second product, Volta Flow, a nasal inhaler. One shark offered the EGP 500,000 for 10% they had asked for. Mohamed Farouk offered EGP 1 million for 30%, which the show called his fastest offer ever, and that is the one recorded here.
