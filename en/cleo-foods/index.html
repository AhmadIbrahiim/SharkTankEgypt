In short: Cleo Foods pitched in Season 3, episode 16. The founders asked for 5M EGP for 15%, valuing the company at 33.3M EGP. They left without a deal.

Overview

Cleo Foods makes rice-cake snacks as a healthy alternative to bread, adapted to Egyptian tastes. Its range spans plain, salted, cinnamon, cheese and chili rice cakes plus a gluten-free Cleo Minis line, sold through supermarkets such as Carrefour and Metro and on Talabat's grocery service. It had 36 products and EGP 15 million in 2024 sales. Founder Ahmed Ismail asked for EGP 5 million for 15% and held firm as the sharks pressed to invest. It left the tank without a deal.
