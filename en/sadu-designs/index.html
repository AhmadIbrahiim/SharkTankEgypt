Overview

Sadu Designs is an Egyptian home-décor and handmade-furniture brand that brings heritage crafts into modern homes. Founded by Ahmed Abdel Rasoul and Fatma Wagdy, it pairs traditional handcraft techniques with locally sourced materials to create pieces with real character. The brand answers growing demand in Egypt for authentic handicrafts while supporting local craftsmanship. It went on to close a deal with Mohamed Farouk on Shark Tank Egypt.

The pitch

Founders Ahmed Abdel Rasoul and Fatma Wagdy pitched Sadu Designs in Season 3 as a heritage-inspired home-décor and handmade-furniture brand riding Egypt's rising demand for handicrafts. They left the tank with a deal from Mohamed Farouk.
