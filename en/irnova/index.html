In short: Irnova pitched in Season 3, episode 11. The founders asked for 15M EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 150M EGP. They left without a deal.

Overview

Irnova (stylised LRNOVA) is a Cairo-based edtech startup that uses AI to turn traditional curricula and training materials into engaging, interactive digital content. Schools, universities and training providers often lack the time and budget to film courses, so the web platform produces educational videos and full courses presented by culturally tailored virtual avatars. It supports Arabic and other languages and adds a virtual tutor for learners. The startup later won a strategic investment from EdVentures, Nahdet Misr Group's venture arm, to expand into Saudi Arabia and the Gulf.

  • AI platform that turns curricula into video courses
  • Lessons presented by culturally tailored virtual avatars
  • Supports Arabic plus multiple languages, with a virtual tutor
  • Backed by EdVentures (Nahdet Misr Group) via EdTech Venture Studio
  • Expanding into Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf

The pitch

In a memorable entrance, the Irnova team appeared in the tank as AI-generated virtual avatars, a live demo of what their platform produces. They pitched their AI tool for creating educational videos and courses and asked for EGP 15 million. The sharks were impressed by the avatar showcase, but the team left without a deal.

Services & products

  • AI educational video generationTurns lesson content into educational videos presented by AI virtual avatars, with no filming needed.
  • Curriculum-to-course conversionConverts traditional curricula and training materials into structured, interactive digital courses.
  • Culturally customised avatarsVirtual presenters tailored to local culture and audiences across Egypt and the Gulf.
  • Arabic and multilingual contentProduces learning content in Arabic and several other languages.
