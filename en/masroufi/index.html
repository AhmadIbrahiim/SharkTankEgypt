Overview

Masroofi ("my pocket money") is an Egyptian fintech that gives children a prepaid card and a money-learning app, so kids aged roughly 6 to 15 can spend, save and pay electronically while their parents stay in control. Parents set up a profile, top up the wallet from their own bank card and order an NFC card for their child (priced at EGP 75 in January 2023 coverage). Launched in January 2022 as Egypt's first e-wallet built for children, it issues its cards with Arab Bank and Visa and raised a $1.5 million seed round in 2023.

  • Billed as Egypt's first e-wallet built for children
  • Cards issued with Arab Bank, plus an agreement with Visa
  • Raised a $1.5M seed round in 2023
  • Aims to reach 2 million children within five years

The pitch

Co-founders Mostafa Abdel-Khabeer and Sayed Hosni pitched Masroofi in Season 1, and turned down a EGP 16 million offer from the sharks. They presented their prepaid card and app as a way to bring children into cashless payments safely under parental supervision. The founders left the tank without a deal.

Services & products

  • Kids' prepaid NFC cardA prepaid card for children to make cashless payments under parental supervision.
  • Parent app and walletParents create a profile, top up the child's wallet from their own bank card and supervise spending.
  • Financial-learning featuresIn-app tools that help kids learn to spend, save and earn money responsibly.
  • Masroofi POS networkPoint-of-sale machines at partner locations where children can pay with their Masroofi card.
