Overview

ThinkTree is an Egyptian edtech startup, founded in 2024 by Ahmed El-Adly, that teaches chess to children and teens online. It uses the game to build thinking skills and character. Students join live online sessions, either in small groups or one-to-one with a coach. They follow a structured 13-level curriculum taught in Arabic or English, and plans start at about $11 a month. ThinkTree has taught students from Egypt, the UAE, Pakistan, the UK and the US.

  • Live online chess classes for children and teens
  • Structured 13-level curriculum in Arabic and English
  • Students from Egypt, the UAE, Pakistan, the UK and the US
  • Plans start at about $11 a month
  • Deal with Mohamed Mansour and Dina Ghabbour: EGP 2M for 30%

The pitch

Ahmed El-Adly pitched ThinkTree on Season 4 of Shark Tank Egypt. He presented chess as a way to build children's thinking skills and character, and showed the platform's live online classes and its students from several countries. He closed a deal with Mohamed Mansour and Dina Ghabbour for EGP 2 million in exchange for 30% of the company.

Services & products

  • Group chess classesLive online chess lessons for kids in small groups, led by a coach.
  • Private one-to-one classesPersonal live sessions with a coach, matched to each child's level and pace.
  • 13-level chess curriculumA structured path that takes children from their first moves to advanced play.
  • Bilingual teachingClasses are taught in Arabic or English for families in Egypt and abroad.
