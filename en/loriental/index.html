Overview

L'Oriental is an Egyptian premium catering company that serves weddings and other special events. It helps couples, families and event hosts who want high-quality food and polished service on their big day, without having to coordinate the kitchen and service themselves. The brand runs under the name L'Oriental Moments on social media and takes bookings online and through WhatsApp. It pitched on Shark Tank Egypt in Season 2, Episode 10.

The pitch

L'Oriental pitched its premium wedding and event catering business to the sharks in Season 2, Episode 10 of Shark Tank Egypt. Details of the ask, the sharks' reactions and the outcome have not been confirmed.
