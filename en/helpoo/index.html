In short: Helpoo pitched in Season 4, episode 2. The founders asked for 40M EGP for 25%, valuing the company at 160M EGP. They left without a deal.

Overview

Helpoo is a Cairo-based roadside-assistance app founded in 2022 and billed as Egypt's first officially licensed provider of smart roadside help, holding a licence from the Financial Regulatory Authority. Drivers request towing, tyre changes, battery boosts or fuel delivery through the app or the 24/7 hotline 17000, see the price upfront based on distance, and follow the camera-equipped rescue truck live on a map. The app also lets users report an accident to their insurer on the spot. In 2023 Saudi mobility platform Morni invested in Helpoo.

  • Licensed by Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA)
  • 24/7 hotline 17000 alongside iOS and Android apps
  • Upfront distance-based pricing and live truck tracking
  • Saudi mobility platform Morni invested in 2023, via Exits MENA

The pitch

Helpoo's founders asked for EGP 40M for 25% of the company, pitching a licensed, tech-enabled roadside-assistance service with live truck tracking and in-app accident reporting. A heated debate followed, with the sharks calling the model traditional, and the founders left without a deal.

Services & products

  • TowingOn-demand tow trucks with cameras and GPS trackers, priced upfront by distance and trackable live on the map.
  • Tyre changeA technician comes to the car to change or fix a flat tyre wherever the driver is stuck.
  • Battery boostRoadside jump-start or battery charging for cars that won't start.
  • Fuel deliveryFuel brought to drivers who have run out on the road.
  • Accident reporting to insurerReport an accident to your insurance company from the app, right at the scene.
  • 24/7 hotline 17000Request any service by phone around the clock if you'd rather not use the app.
