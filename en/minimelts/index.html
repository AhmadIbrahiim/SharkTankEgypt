In short: Mini Melts pitched in Season 3, episode 5. The founders asked for 30M EGP for 20%, valuing the company at 150M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Mohamed Farouk and Ahmed Tarek Khalil.

Overview

Mini Melts Egypt holds the exclusive licence to produce Mini Melts, the tiny frozen beads of ice cream, in Egypt and Africa. In Cairo it sells from spots including City Stars, Masaken Al Mohandesin and Nasr City. In summer its trucks and kiosks serve North Coast destinations such as La Vista Bay, Amwaj, Mountain View, Hacienda Bay and Telal. On Shark Tank, Mohamed Farouk and Ahmed Tarek backed the idea and invested; the terms were not reported.
