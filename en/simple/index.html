In short: Sympl pitched in Season 2, episode 1. The founders asked for 15M EGP for 2%, valuing the company at 750M EGP. They left without a deal.

Overview

Sympl is an Egyptian payment platform that lets shoppers split purchases into short-term, interest-free payments at participating stores and websites. Customers choose a payment plan, pay a one-time service fee and track their payment dates in the app. Its official FAQ states that the bank-card balance must cover the full purchase amount at checkout. The platform also partners with retailers to offer instalments to their customers.

  • Raised a $6M seed round led by BECO Capital, with A15 and Global Ventures
  • Accepted at 240+ retail and online stores within months of launch
  • Targeted EGP 800M in sales by end of 2024 and EGP 1B by end of 2025

The pitch

Sympl came to the Shark Tank Egypt stage in Season 2, Episode 1, pitching a buy-now-pay-later platform that brings instalment payments to everyday Egyptian shoppers. The founders presented the product as a simpler, fairer alternative to traditional consumer credit. The episode ended without a deal.

Services & products

  • In-store instalmentsSplit purchases at partner retail stores into instalments right at checkout.
  • Online checkout BNPLPay-later option for shoppers buying from partner e-commerce stores.
  • Payment trackingUse the app to view purchases and payment due dates.
  • Merchant partnershipsRetailers add Sympl as a payment option to boost sales and basket size.
  • Health and education paymentsInstalment plans for services beyond retail, such as healthcare and education.
