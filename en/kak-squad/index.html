Overview

KAK Squad is a fried-chicken and burger fast-food brand with its own integrated factory. Its menu mixes classic fried chicken with Asian-inspired sauces such as Thai chilli, smokehouse, honey and teriyaki, and its signature dip and sauced meals come in 2 to 9 pieces; in 2026 EnterpriseAM's "Eat This Tonight" column named it a top pick for fried chicken in Cairo. It grew from a small restaurant and entered the tank with the goal of making Egyptian fried chicken compete globally. The sharks questioned it about its recipes and the international cooking standards it follows.

  • Fried chicken and burgers with its own factory
  • Asian-inspired sauces like Thai chilli and teriyaki
  • Dip and sauced meals from 2 to 9 pieces
  • Praised by EnterpriseAM in 2026
