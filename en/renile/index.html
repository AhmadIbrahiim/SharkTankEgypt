In short: ReNile pitched in Season 3, episode 8. The founders asked for 15M EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 150M EGP. They left without a deal.

Overview

ReNile is an agritech company offering end-to-end smart-farming systems that use IoT sensors to monitor water, air and soil quality around the clock, send instant alerts about problems and suggest fixes, with remote control and data analytics. Its clients span greenhouses, hydroponics, aquaponics, fish and poultry farms and traditional farms. Founder Hazem El Tawab asked for EGP 15 million for 10% but left without a deal; in 2025 ReNile raised $450,000 and announced plans for a Saudi subsidiary.
