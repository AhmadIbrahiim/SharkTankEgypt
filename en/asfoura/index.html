In short: Asfoura pitched in Season 1, episode 11. The founders asked for 2.5M EGP for 20%, valuing the company at 12.5M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Abdallah Sallam, Ahmed El Sewedy and Mohamed Farouk: 2.5M EGP for 30%.

Overview

Asfoura is the first Egyptian publisher dedicated to children's books and stories written in Egyptian colloquial Arabic, founded in Cairo in 2021 to help children build their spoken Arabic in a familiar, unintimidating way. Many of its titles revolve around Egyptian food, such as Waraa Shagar, about two friends cooking molokhia, and Kahk El-Eid, about a boy baking kahk with his grandmother, and it pairs the books with hands-on cooking workshops. It sold about 3,200 books in its latest year. The sharks competed fiercely to win over founder Maram Khamis, and she accepted a joint offer of EGP 2.5 million for 30% from Abdallah Sallam, Ahmed El Sewedy and Mohamed Farouk.
