Overview

Right Temp is an Egyptian cooling-solutions company founded by Karim Taha. It makes containers that hold products such as food at a set temperature without needing any power source, which is useful wherever electricity or refrigeration is unavailable or unreliable. The containers come in several types and sizes to suit different loads and uses. Right Temp brought its passive cooling idea to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 3.

The pitch

Founder Karim Taha pitched Right Temp's power-free temperature-controlled containers in Season 3, Episode 13 of Shark Tank Egypt. He asked for EGP 4 million in exchange for 10% of the company, which values it at EGP 40 million. No public source confirms the outcome yet.
