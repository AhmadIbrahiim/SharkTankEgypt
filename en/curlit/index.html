In short: Curlit pitched in Season 3, episode 1. The founders asked for 2M EGP for 20%, valuing the company at 10M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Dina Ghabbour and Mohamed Farouk: 5M EGP for 50%.

Overview

Curlit is an Egyptian hair-care brand made for curly and wavy hair, started by Alia Osman after she struggled to find products for her own curls. Osman, who holds a diploma in beauty-product manufacturing, launched it with Mohamed Attya, and its curl creams, shampoos, conditioners, gels and leave-ins are formulated in-house for all curl patterns and all genders. Egyptian Streets praised its minimalist formulas and affordable prices as a local alternative to imported products. In the tank she asked for EGP 2 million for 20% and accepted EGP 5 million for 50% from Dina Ghabbour and Mohamed Farouk, dropping to 30% once the capital is recouped.

  • Curl creams, shampoos, conditioners, gels and leave-ins
  • Formulated in-house for all curl patterns and genders
  • Leave-ins for both curly and wavy hair
