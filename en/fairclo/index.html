Overview

Virclo is an Egyptian online marketplace built around a simple idea: the things you no longer need still have value to someone else. Users can list any unwanted product and choose to sell it, buy from others, or swap it for something they actually want, keeping usable items in circulation instead of in storage. Founded by Mohamed El Kholy, Virclo pitched in Season 1 of Shark Tank Egypt and closed a deal with Ayman Abbas and Ahmed El Sewedy.

The pitch

Founder Mohamed El Kholy pitched Virclo, a marketplace for selling, buying and swapping unwanted products, in Season 1, Episode 4, asking for EGP 2 million for 5% equity. He left with a deal from Ayman Abbas and Ahmed El Sewedy for the full EGP 2 million, but in exchange for 30% of the company.
