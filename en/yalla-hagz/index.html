Overview

Yalla Hagz is an Egyptian app for booking football pitches, padel courts and padbol courts in a few taps, so players don't have to make calls or turn up hoping a pitch is free. Each venue page shows the pitch size, surface, amenities and player ratings, along with live availability. Bookings are confirmed instantly and the app sends reminders before kick-off. A team of five young founders, aged 17 to 22, started it and is aiming for 500 booked hours a day.

  • Books football, padel and padbol courts across Egypt
  • Live availability with instant booking confirmation
  • Venue size, surface, amenities and ratings shown
  • Founded by five founders aged 17 to 22
  • Targeting 500 booked hours a day

The pitch

The five young founders of Yalla Hagz, aged 17 to 22, asked for EGP 1 million for 10% of the company, valuing it at EGP 10 million. They pitched their pitch-booking app and their goal of 500 booked hours a day. The sharks gave them blunt feedback on the pitch. No public source confirms the outcome yet.
