Overview

OG Bees is an Egyptian platform for producing and managing user-generated content (UGC) for brands. Instead of relying only on big influencers or studio shoots, it turns everyday social-media users into paid content creators who make authentic posts and videos for brands. Brands get a steady flow of real, relatable content, while ordinary users get a way to earn from what they already do online. The startup pitched on Season 4 of Shark Tank Egypt, where several sharks competed for the deal. Ayman Abbas invested; the show listed it among his three strongest Season 4 investments.

The pitch

The founder pitched OG Bees as a way to turn ordinary social-media users into paid UGC creators for brands. Mohamed Farouk made an offer, but Ahmed Tarek advised the founder not to accept it, and the sharks ended up competing for the deal. The show's official recap for Season 4, Episode 19 confirms an on-air agreement with Ayman Abbas for EGP 5 million in exchange for 17.5% equity.

Services & products

  • UGC production for brandsBrands commission authentic user-generated posts and videos made by real social-media users.
  • UGC campaign managementThe platform manages the content flow between brands and creators, from brief to delivered content.
  • Paid creator programEveryday social-media users join as creators and get paid for the content they produce for brands.
