Overview

Churrito is an Egyptian dessert brand built around Spanish-style churros, the crisp fried-dough sticks traditionally served with sugar and dipping sauces. Founded by Nourhan Hany, the brand set out to bring this Spanish street-food favourite to Egyptian dessert lovers. Churrito came to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 4 looking for investment to help it expand.

The pitch

Founder Nourhan Hany pitched Churrito, her Spanish churros dessert brand, in Season 4, Episode 8 of Shark Tank Egypt. She asked the sharks for EGP 3 million to fund the brand's expansion. No public source confirms the outcome yet.
