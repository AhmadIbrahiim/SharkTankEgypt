Who is Mohamed El Hafez?

Mohamed El Hafez is a founder of Mandoobak Smart. Mandobak Smart helps grocery and supermarket owners order stock through one mobile app. Its founders presented the service on Shark Tank Egypt as a way to save retailers time and effort, with delivery included.

What company did Mohamed El Hafez pitch on Shark Tank Egypt?

Mandoobak Smart: Mandoobak Smart is an app that lets grocers and supermarket owners order everything their shops need from one page, at company prices and with free delivery, saving them time and effort.
