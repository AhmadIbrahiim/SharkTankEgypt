About

Mina Mandaloun is a communications engineer who co-founded By Pavo with Khaled El Boraay, a colleague he met while both worked at Orange. He designs the brand's bags. The Egyptian brand was born from the lack of affordable, locally made luggage and makes minimalist bags, backpacks and footwear.
