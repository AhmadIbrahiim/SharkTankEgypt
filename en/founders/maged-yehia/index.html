Frequently asked questions

Who is Maged Yehia?

Maged Yehia founded Mavericks, a design business that turns reclaimed materials into lighting, furniture and corporate gifts. On Shark Tank Egypt, he described combining Egyptian heritage with contemporary design and reported more than 500 designs and 3,000 finished pieces.

What company did Maged Yehia pitch on Shark Tank Egypt?

Mavericks: Mavericks is an Egyptian art and design studio that upcycles waste, mainly scrap metal, into lighting units, furniture, art pieces and corporate gifts.
