Overview

RoboDesk is an Egyptian software company, described as an official Meta partner, that runs an AI-powered contact-centre platform for large and small businesses. It brings customer conversations into one system and uses AI to automate replies and support. Founded in 2019 and based in Nasr City, Cairo, it came to the tank with founder Amir Ali asking for EGP 43 million for 20%. It was one of three Season 3 pitches that brought all five sharks together in a single investment.
