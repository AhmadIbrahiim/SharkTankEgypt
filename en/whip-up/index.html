In short: Whip Up pitched in Season 3, episode 9. The founders asked for 25M EGP for 20%, valuing the company at 125M EGP. They walked away with a deal: 25M EGP for 35%.

Overview

Whip Up is an Egyptian food-processing company that turns fresh shell eggs into ready-to-use egg products. Its factory separates egg whites from yolks and reprocesses them into pasteurized products tailored to specific uses. The goal is to give food businesses a safer, more convenient alternative to cracking eggs by hand, with a long shelf life, high hygiene standards and easy storage. Founder Tarek Amir brought the company to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 3.

The pitch

In Season 3, Episode 9, founder Tarek Amir pitched Whip Up's egg-processing factory, which separates whites from yolks and reprocesses eggs into pasteurized products. He asked for EGP 25 million in exchange for 20% of the company. He closed a deal at EGP 25 million for 35%, with a time-limited royalty.

Services & products

  • Pasteurized egg whitesEgg whites separated from the yolk and pasteurized for safe, ready-to-use cooking and baking.
  • Pasteurized egg yolksSeparated yolks processed and pasteurized for food production that needs yolk only.
  • Customized egg productsEgg products tailored to a client's specific usage, with long shelf life and easy storage.
