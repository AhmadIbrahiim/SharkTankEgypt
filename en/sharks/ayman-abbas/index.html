Ayman Abbas

Founder of Egyptian Chinese Drilling Company; Chairman of ADES

Seasons: Season 1Season 2Season 3Season 4Season 5

Ayman Abbas is an Egyptian businessman whose career is rooted in the oil and gas drilling sector. He founded the Egyptian Chinese Drilling Company in 1998 and chairs ADES International Holding, a contract drilling group operating across the Middle East and Africa. He heads several enterprises and has served as non-executive chairman of Rameda, a listed Egyptian pharmaceutical company. A founding shark on Shark Tank Egypt and a member of the panel in every season, he is known for backing young founders, including co-investing with Abdallah Sallam in a teenage contestant's checkout startup.
