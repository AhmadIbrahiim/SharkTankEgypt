Ahmed El Sewedy

President & CEO of Elsewedy Electric

Seasons: Season 1Season 2Season 3Season 4

Ahmed Sadek El Sewedy is the President, CEO and Managing Director of Elsewedy Electric, the family-founded Egyptian group that began as a small Cairo trading business in 1938. An electrical engineering graduate of Cairo University, he joined the board in 2005 and led the group's transformation into a multinational provider of cables, electrical products, energy and infrastructure projects. Under his leadership the company grew to dozens of production facilities, operations in around 50 countries and some 22,000 employees, with major expansions across Africa and the Gulf. A founding shark on Shark Tank Egypt, he brings deep industrial and export experience to the panel.
