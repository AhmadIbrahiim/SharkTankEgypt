Noor Sweid

Founder & Managing Partner of Global Ventures

Seasons: Season 3Season 4

Noor Sweid is a venture capitalist and the founder and managing partner of Global Ventures, a UAE-based firm founded in 2018 that backs Series A startups across the Middle East and Africa, with a presence in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Nigeria. She was previously chief investment officer of the Dubai Future Foundation. Forbes Middle East lists her among the region's top venture capitalists and most powerful businesswomen. She has been an investor on Shark Tank Dubai and appeared on the Shark Tank Egypt panel in season four.
