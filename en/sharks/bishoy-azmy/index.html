Bishoy Azmy

CEO of ASGC

Seasons: Season 4

Bishoy Azmy is an Egyptian construction executive and the CEO of ASGC, a Dubai-based construction and infrastructure group. He joined ASGC as a site engineer and rose through the ranks to become CEO in 2008, overseeing the group's growth into a major contractor across the Gulf and Egypt. An alumnus of the American University in Cairo, he became the youngest member of AUC's Board of Trustees in December 2020. He joined the Shark Tank Egypt panel in season four as an investor supporting Egyptian entrepreneurs.
