Ahmed Tarek Khalil

Founder of Allianz Marine Services; co-founder of NextEra Education

Seasons: Season 2Season 3Season 4Season 5

Ahmed Tarek Khalil is an Egyptian shipping entrepreneur and investor who founded Allianz Marine Services in 2012. He has since diversified into education, co-founding NextEra Education, which acquires and expands private schools in Egypt, and has announced plans to invest around $100 million in healthcare. He also holds stakes in listed companies and has been active in public-market investing. He joined Shark Tank Egypt as a shark and has become one of the show's prominent investors in recent seasons.
