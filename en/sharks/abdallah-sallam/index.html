Abdallah Sallam

President & CEO of Madinet Masr

Seasons: Season 1Season 2Season 3Season 4Season 5

Abdallah Sallam is an Egyptian real-estate executive and the President and CEO of Madinet Masr, one of Egypt's oldest developers, founded in 1959. He previously founded the development firm Minka, which Madinet Masr acquired in October 2021, when he was appointed to lead the combined company. Under his leadership Madinet Masr has expanded rapidly, reporting contracted sales of about $810 million in 2024, and Forbes Middle East lists him among the region's most impactful real-estate leaders. A founding shark on Shark Tank Egypt and one of the show's best-known faces, he also mentors founders through platforms such as Almentor.
