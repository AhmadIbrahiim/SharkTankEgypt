Mohamed Farouk

Chairman & CEO of Mobica

Seasons: Season 1Season 2Season 3Season 4Season 5

Mohamed Farouk is the Chairman and CEO of Mobica Designs, one of Egypt's best-known furniture manufacturers. Founded in 1979, Mobica helped transform the Egyptian furniture industry, specialising in office and contract furniture for corporate and public-sector clients. Farouk has led the company's growth in design, manufacturing and regional exports. A founding shark on Shark Tank Egypt, he is known on the show for founder-friendly gestures, such as offering to manufacture a young contestant's product stands for free without taking any equity.
