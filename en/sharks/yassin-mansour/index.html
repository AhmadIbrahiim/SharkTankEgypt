Yasseen Mansour

Chairman & Group CEO of Palm Hills Developments

Seasons: Season 3

Yasseen Mansour is an Egyptian billionaire businessman and the founder, chairman and group CEO of Palm Hills Developments, which he established in 1997. Under his leadership Palm Hills grew from a single project into one of Egypt's leading real-estate developers, with a land bank of about 38 million square metres across Egypt and the UAE. He is also a board member of the Mansour Group, with a career spanning real estate, automotive, construction and food and beverage. He joined Shark Tank Egypt as a guest shark in season three.
