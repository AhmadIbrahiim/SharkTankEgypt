Mohamed Ismail Mansour

Co-founder & Chairman of Infinity Group and Infinity Power

Seasons: Season 1Season 2Season 3Season 4

Mohamed Ismail Mansour is an Egyptian renewable-energy investor who co-founded Infinity Group in 2014 and chairs Infinity Power, its joint venture with Abu Dhabi's Masdar. Infinity Power develops solar, wind and battery-storage projects and operates about 1.3 GW across Egypt, South Africa and Senegal, targeting 10 GW in Africa by 2030. He led its 2023 acquisition of Lekela Power, described as Africa's biggest renewable-energy deal, and is deputy chairman of Palm Hills Developments. Forbes Middle East lists him among the region's sustainability leaders. He was a founding shark on Shark Tank Egypt.
