Overview

Stunning (stunning.so), founded by Mohamed Ghaith in 2023, is an AI platform that turns an idea described in plain language, spoken or typed, into a website, app or dashboard without code. It generates layouts, images and copy, and adds built-in SEO, AI marketing tools, CRM features and multi-site management for agencies. It natively supports Arabic and right-to-left layouts and serves freelancers, agencies and businesses, with a strong focus on the MENA region. Ghaith came to it after four failed ventures; in the tank the sharks asked how it differs from Google, and the episode and its EGP 12.5M deal with Ahmed Tarek Khalil later stirred public debate.
