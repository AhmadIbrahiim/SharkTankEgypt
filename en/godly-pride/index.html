In short: Godly Pride pitched in Season 5, episode 2. The founders asked for 3M EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 30M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Mohamed Farouk and Hala El-Kasem: 3M EGP for 25%.

Overview

Godly Pride is an Egyptian beauty brand founded by Maryam Elleithy. It combines skincare and makeup in products such as facial cleansers and cosmetics. Maryam said the business sold 48,000 units and generated more than EGP 14 million in sales over three and a half years before its Shark Tank Egypt pitch.

The pitch

In Season 5, Episode 2, Maryam Elleithy asked for EGP 3 million for 10%, implying a EGP 30 million valuation. She described the brand's skincare-infused makeup, 48,000 units sold and more than EGP 14 million in cumulative sales. The show's official recap and reel confirm an on-air agreement with Mohamed Farouk and Hala El-Kasem for EGP 3 million in exchange for 25% equity.
