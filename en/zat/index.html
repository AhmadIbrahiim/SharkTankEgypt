Overview

ZAT (ذات, Arabic for 'self') is an Egyptian cosmetics and skincare brand for women, built on natural, science-backed formulas. Its philosophy celebrates real beauty rather than chasing unrealistic standards, offering women everyday products made with natural ingredients. Founder Alaa Mahfouz started ZAT during a period out of work and turned a personal hobby into a business. The brand gained national exposure on Shark Tank Egypt, where shark Abdallah Sallam saw its potential and invested.

  • Egyptian natural skincare and cosmetics brand for women
  • Philosophy built around celebrating real beauty
  • Founder turned a personal hobby into a business
  • Backed by shark Abdallah Sallam on Shark Tank Egypt

The pitch

Founder Alaa Mahfouz pitched ZAT on Shark Tank Egypt as a natural, science-backed skincare and cosmetics brand that celebrates real beauty, born from a hobby she turned into a business. Abdallah Sallam quickly recognised the brand's potential and invested in ZAT.
