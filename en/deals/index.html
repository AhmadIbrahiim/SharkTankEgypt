How these numbers are calculated

  • A deal is an agreement made on air and stated by a public source; whether the investment later closed legally is not tracked.
  • Disclosed investment sums only the deals whose amount is public, so it is a floor, not a total.
  • Valuation = amount ÷ (equity ÷ 100), shown only when both are known.
  • * Deal rate = deals ÷ pitches with a known outcome; unconfirmed pitches count as neither deal nor no-deal.
  • Each figure states how many deals it covers; unknown values are left out, never estimated.

Valuation calculator

Post-money valuation = amount ÷ (equity ÷ 100); pre-money valuation = post-money − amount.
