Overview

Creative Art is an Egyptian designer furniture brand founded by Hany Bahaa, best known for its water-resistant tables. It sets out to combine artistic, statement design with everyday practicality, so a decorative piece can stand up to spills and moisture in a real home. The brand pitched on the first season of Shark Tank Egypt, bringing its signature tables to the sharks to show how design-led furniture can also be durable.

The pitch

Founder Hany Bahaa brought Creative Art to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 1, Episode 7, presenting the brand's water-resistant designer tables. The ask, the sharks' reactions and the outcome were not found in available sources, and no deal is on record.
