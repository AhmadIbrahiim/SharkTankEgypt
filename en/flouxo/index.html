In short: Flouxo pitched in Season 2, episode 14. The founders asked for 6M EGP for 30%, valuing the company at 20M EGP. They walked away with a deal for 50%.

Overview

Flouxo is an Egyptian manufacturer of compressors that unload bulk cement from transport trucks. It describes itself as the first maker of this kind of compressor in Egypt and the Middle East, giving truck operators a local option instead of imported equipment. Its design is meant to spare truck owners the cost of frequent compressor overhauls, which keeps their fleets running for less. Founder Hisham Hany brought the business to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 2. On the show, founder Hisham Hany closed a deal with two sharks for 50% of the company.

  • Claims to be Egypt's and the Middle East's first bulk-cement compressor maker
  • Design cuts compressor overhaul costs for truck owners
  • Asked EGP 6M for 30% on Shark Tank Egypt Season 2

The pitch

In Season 2, Episode 14, founder Hisham Hany pitched Flouxo as the first Egyptian and Middle Eastern maker of compressors for unloading bulk cement trucks, built to cut overhaul costs for truck owners. He asked for EGP 6 million for 30% of the company. According to the show summary, he ended up giving 50% to two sharks.
