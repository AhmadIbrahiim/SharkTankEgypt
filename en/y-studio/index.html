In short: Y Studios pitched in Specials, episode 2. The founders asked for 1.5M EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 15M EGP. They walked away with a deal: 1.5M EGP for 15%.

Overview

Y Studios is an Egyptian fashion brand founded by Youssef El Sherif. It makes simple, contemporary clothing inspired by European styles, with prices aimed at the local market. In the second special episode of Shark Tank Egypt, Youssef asked for EGP 1.5 million for 10% and agreed to EGP 1.5 million for 15%.
