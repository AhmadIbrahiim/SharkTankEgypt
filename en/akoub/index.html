Overview

Akoub AgriTech Services is an Egyptian agritech startup that uses agricultural drones, artificial intelligence and data analysis to pollinate date palms and spray pesticides. A drone can pollinate about 160 palms a day, compared with roughly 40 by hand, and the company targets Egypt's approximately 15 million palm trees. Its work also covers detecting palm diseases such as the red palm weevil and selling dates. Akoub was among the entries in the startup track of Ain Shams University's Innovate 2023 competition.
