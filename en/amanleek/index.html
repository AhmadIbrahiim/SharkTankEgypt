Overview

Amanleek is an online insurance broker that lets customers compare medical and car insurance quotes from different insurers from home in three steps, serving both individuals and businesses. Founded in 2019 and based in New Cairo, it is described as the first Egyptian insurtech to hold a digital brokerage licence and offers its own medical plans such as Secure Plus. It has 30,000+ clients and EGP 185 million in sales. Founder Mohab Abou Eita asked for EGP 40 million for 10%. It left the tank without a deal.

  • Compare medical and car insurance in three steps
