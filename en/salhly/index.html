Overview

Salhly (Sal7lyy) is described as the first home-maintenance company in Upper Egypt, based in Asyut. Its app connects households, as well as businesses such as hotels, clinics and schools, with a network of about 100 vetted technicians for electrical, plumbing, carpentry, air-conditioning and satellite-receiver work. Customers get a free inspection, a fixed price agreed upfront, original spare parts from an in-app store, online payment and a 30-day guarantee. It serves Asyut, Sohag and New Valley, and the Athar Foundation named it the best startup in Upper Egypt.

  • Described as Upper Egypt's first home-maintenance company

The pitch

In Season 2, Episode 13, Salhly pitched itself to the Sharks as Upper Egypt's first organised home-maintenance service: an app that matches customers in Asyut and neighbouring governorates with about 100 technicians. The founders positioned it as a trusted, fixed-price alternative to informal repairmen in a region big platforms barely serve. No source we found gives the amount asked for or the outcome.

Services & products

  • Electrical repairsBook a qualified electrician through the app for home or business wiring and fixtures.
  • PlumbingOn-demand plumbers for leaks, installations and repairs, at a fixed price agreed upfront.
  • CarpentryCarpentry and furniture repair carried out by vetted local technicians.
  • AC & satellite receiversAir-conditioning servicing and satellite receiver installation and tuning.
  • In-app spare parts storeBuy original spare parts directly in the app, delivered with the service visit.
  • Guarantee & free inspectionFree inspection before the job, online payment, follow-up and a 30-day guarantee on all work.
