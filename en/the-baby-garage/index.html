In short: The Baby Garage pitched in Season 3, episode 11. The founders asked for 9M EGP for 10%, valuing the company at 90M EGP. They walked away with a deal.

Overview

The Baby Garage is a marketplace where parents can buy, sell or donate new and used children's gear, aimed at new mothers unsure what to buy or where to buy it. Farah Ahmed launched it in 2015 as a volunteer-run community, and it has grown into a business based in 6th of October City that earns from merchant subscriptions, sales commissions, a discount card and ad campaigns for baby brands. Its "Mompreneur" initiative has helped more than a thousand mothers start reselling from home, and the company says its community counts more than 520,000 mothers. A TIEC incubator graduate and winner of the Al-Forsa TV competition, it asked the sharks for EGP 9 million for 10%. On the show it closed a deal.
