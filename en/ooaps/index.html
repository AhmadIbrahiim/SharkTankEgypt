Overview

OOAPS (also written Ooaaps) is an Egyptian digital platform that makes importing and exporting easier for traders and businesses. Cross-border trade in Egypt involves a long chain of paperwork and customs procedures, so the platform breaks the process into seven clear steps and handles customs clearance electronically. Customers can also track their shipments, manage inventory and keep financial records in one place. Founded in 2020 by Moamen Ragab, the company has reported plans for a parcel warehouse at Alexandria Port and a customer base of around 3,000.

The pitch

In Season 3, Episode 11, founder Moamen Ragab presented OOAPS as a way to cut Egypt's complicated import and export procedures down to seven simple steps. He asked the sharks for EGP 7 million in exchange for 20% of the company. No public source confirms the outcome yet.

Services & products

  • Digital customs clearanceElectronic handling of customs clearance for companies importing into or exporting from Egypt.
  • Seven-step trade processImport and export procedures condensed into seven guided steps for traders and businesses.
  • Shipment trackingFollow the status of import and export shipments from the platform.
  • Inventory managementTools to manage the stock that moves through a client's import and export operations.
  • Financial recordsCompile and keep the financial records linked to each trade operation in one place.
