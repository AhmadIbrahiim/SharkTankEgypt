In short: Aqar Exit pitched in Season 5, episode 2. The founders asked for 10M EGP for 5%, valuing the company at 200M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Ayman Abbas: 10M EGP for 10%.

Overview

Aqar Exit is an Egyptian platform for reselling property units and transferring purchase contracts. It connects owners who need to leave installment commitments with buyers seeking units under earlier contracts. Founder Mahmoud Ammar said the platform had listed about 8,000 units in its first 40 days; this is a count of listings, not completed sales.

The pitch

In Season 5, Episode 2, Mahmoud Ammar asked for EGP 10 million for 5%, implying a EGP 200 million valuation. A post-air report says Ayman Abbas agreed to EGP 10 million for 10%, split into EGP 7 million in 2026 and EGP 3 million in 2027. That implies a EGP 100 million valuation on the reported terms. The report describes an on-air agreement; completion of the investment has not been independently confirmed.
