About the show

What is Shark Tank Egypt?

Shark Tank Egypt (شارك تانك مصر) is the Egyptian edition of the Shark Tank / Dragons' Den format, aired on CBC and WATCH IT since 2023. Founders pitch their startups to a panel of investors ("sharks") and ask for money in exchange for equity.

How many seasons of Shark Tank Egypt are there?

5 seasons so far (2023 to 2026), plus a set of special episodes with Robert Herjavec in July 2025.

Who are the sharks on Shark Tank Egypt?

17 investors have sat on the panel: Ayman Abbas, Mohamed Farouk, Ahmed Tarek Khalil, Ahmed El Sewedy, Abdallah Sallam, Dina Ghabbour, Mohamed Ismail Mansour, Noor Sweid, Bishoy Azmy, Hend Sabry, Aya Abdel Raouf, Hala El-Kasem, Hilda Louca, Mona Ataya, Mounaz Abdel Raouf, Yasseen Mansour and Robert Herjavec.
