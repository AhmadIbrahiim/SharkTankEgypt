Overview

Masafa is a Cairo-based online platform, founded in 2022, that acts as a broker for scrap and surplus building materials. Businesses list leftover stock and scrap through the app and other buyers find the materials they need, turning waste into tradeable inventory. CB Insights lists Ahmed Mamdouh as founder and COO, and compares it with recycling marketplaces such as Bekia. On Shark Tank the founders accepted EGP 5 million for 50% plus 10% of profits. Ahmed Mamdouh is COO of Mrkoon, the scrap marketplace StartupScene profiled as a Shark Tank alumnus backed by Ayman Abbas.
