Overview

Cosmos Bionics is an Alexandria startup, founded in 2022, that builds a lightweight powered exoskeleton to help people who have lost mobility stand and walk. The Cosmos is a lower-body hip-and-knee suit driven by motors in the joints and steered by the user shifting their posture; the company says it is the first powered exoskeleton developed in Egypt. It addresses a need it puts at over 2 million Egyptians and 500 million people worldwide. CEO Mina Ashraf's pitch moved the sharks, and the founders closed a deal with Ayman Abbas for EGP 3 million for 10%, earmarked for research and ISO 13485 and CE certification.
