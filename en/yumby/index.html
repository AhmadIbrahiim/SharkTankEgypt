Overview

Yumby is a restaurant-discovery app for organizing food photos, saving favourite places and sharing recommendations with friends. Users can create restaurant lists with dishes and notes, browse a social feed and plan where to eat locally or while travelling. On Shark Tank Egypt, the founders asked for USD 350,000 and left without an investment.
