In short: Hoods pitched in Season 3, episode 16. The founders asked for 5M EGP for 5%, valuing the company at 100M EGP. They walked away with a deal with Ahmed Tarek Khalil and Mona Ataya: 5M EGP for 15%.

Overview

Hoods is a live-shopping app, billed as the first live entertainment-commerce platform in the Middle East, that blends social media and e-commerce and operates in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Shoppers watch live streams, chat with creators and buy in the app, while sellers run live channels without building their own website, payments or shipping. Founded in Cairo in 2020, it raised a $350,000 pre-seed round and backing from valU. Founder Mostafa Hanafy asked for EGP 5 million for 5% and secured EGP 5 million from Ahmed Tarek and Mona Ataya.

  • Live-stream shopping app for sellers and creators
  • Built-in payments and shipping for sellers
  • Raised $350K pre-seed; backed by valU
  • Shark Tank deal: EGP 3.75M for 15% plus EGP 1.25M loan
