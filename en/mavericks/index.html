Overview

Mavericks is an Egyptian art and design studio that upcycles waste, mainly scrap metal, into lighting units, furniture, art pieces and corporate gifts. It gives discarded materials a second life as sustainable design objects that decorate homes, offices and galleries while still serving a practical everyday purpose. The venture was incubated in Nile University's Creative Incubator, which backs creative-industry startups in Egypt.

  • Upcycles scrap metal into functional design pieces
  • Incubated at Nile University's Creative Incubator
  • Serves homes, offices, galleries and corporate clients
  • Pitched on Shark Tank Egypt Season 4

The pitch

Founder Maged Yehia brought Mavericks to Shark Tank Egypt in Season 4, Episode 6, pitching a studio that turns scrap metal into lighting, furniture and art. He asked for EGP 1,000,000 in exchange for 10% equity, valuing the business at EGP 10 million. No public source confirms the outcome yet.

Services & products

  • Upcycled lighting unitsLamps and light fixtures handcrafted from reclaimed scrap metal.
  • Upcycled furnitureFunctional furniture pieces built from discarded metal and other waste materials.
  • Art piecesSculptural decor for homes, offices and galleries made from recycled metal.
  • Corporate giftsSustainable, one-of-a-kind gift pieces produced for companies.
